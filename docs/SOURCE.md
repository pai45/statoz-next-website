Space Grotesk was converted locally from TTF to WOFF2 using fonttools 4.68.0 and Brotli 1.2.1. The delivery font is checked in; these conversion tools are not runtime dependencies. Legacy page styles load through BlogChrome and the download page, and game detail styles load only with game detail routes.

The delivery font contains Latin and common punctuation characters; the complete original TTF remains available. Rebuild instructions are in scripts/prepare-font.py. Orbitron's OFL license is from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/google/fonts/main/ofl/orbitron/OFL.txt and is stored alongside the font.

The Your First Drive entrance uses locally authored Three.js coupe, driver and cockpit geometry, a logo model built from the icon's parts (an image-mapped slab broken into shards, a goal mouth, and the icon's flat octagonal ball, whose outline was measured from app_logo.png and which uses that image as its face), GPU particles sampled from the rendered heading, mission displays, and road/light effects. The gate and App Station lettering use a subset (A–Z, 0–9, space) of Droid Sans Bold from the three.js examples, licensed under Apache 2.0; its notice is stored at public/assets/city/fonts/DROID-NOTICE.txt. The App Station phone draws only public/assets/brand/app-icon.png and statoz-wordmark-v2.png. City sound is synthesised in the browser with WebAudio; no audio files are shipped. The logo texture remains the original asset above. Star Atlas and Igloo Inc. were viewed as interaction and art-direction references; no assets or code from those sites were incorporated. Poster captures and their provenance are generated by scripts/capture-journey.mjs.
